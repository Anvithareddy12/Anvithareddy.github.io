About

The engineer behind the work

Most AI projects die between demo and production. I work on the part that keeps them alive. I'm Anvitha Reddy — an AI Engineer at AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) building production-grade Generative AI systems at the intersection of RAG pipelines, Text-to-SQL, and LLM orchestration at enterprise scale.

At AMD I architected ZenBot — a two-model LLM cascade (OpenAI + Anthropic Claude) that lets non-technical stakeholders self-serve complex queries across 4 enterprise data sources (JIRA, JAMA, ATM, Test Reports) with zero SQL knowledge. The system includes AST-level SQL validation, auto-repair loops, and a RAG pipeline over enterprise report corpora (WHQL, IV, IQE, reliability & declaration reports).

At CornerstoneX I spearheaded a multilingual platform spanning 50+ languages, engineered a CNN-based ASL recognition system achieving 95%+ accuracy at 15 FPS (DHS approved), and contributed to the work that earned the team the NVIDIA GTC 2025 stage and Inception Program selection.

Core stack: Python, LangChain, OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API, Pinecone, and MCP (Model Context Protocol).

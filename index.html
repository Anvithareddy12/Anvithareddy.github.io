About

The engineer behind the work

Most AI projects die between demo and production. I work on the part that keeps them alive. I'm Anvitha Reddy — an AI Engineer at AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) building production Generative AI systems at the intersection of RAG pipelines, Text-to-SQL, and LLM orchestration.

At AMD I designed and shipped ZenBot — a two-model LLM cascade (a lightweight intent classifier routing to a SQL-generation model) that lets non-technical stakeholders self-serve complex queries across 4 previously disconnected systems (JIRA, JAMA, ATM, Reports) with zero SQL knowledge, cutting a recurring 2–4 hour analysis to under 2 minutes. The system pairs parameterized SQL with AST-level validation (sqlglot) and a schema-grounded auto-repair loop for near-zero hallucination, and integrates semantic retrieval (RAG) over report corpora (WHQL, IV, IQE, reliability & declaration reports) into a unified query layer. Accepted as a Software Poster at the AMD Asia Technical Conference (AATC) 2026.

At CornerstoneX I built the real-time multilingual speech engine behind HOPE — a client-funded agentic-AI metahuman spanning 50+ languages — trained a CNN-based ASL gesture-recognition model delivered for a U.S. Department of Homeland Security client, and contributed to the platform presented at NVIDIA GTC 2025 and selected for the NVIDIA Inception Program.

Core stack: Python, SQL, LangChain, OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API, Pinecone, and MCP (Model Context Protocol).
